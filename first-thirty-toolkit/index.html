Inside the workbook

Digestible structure.

Four phases across thirty days

Each page has reflection prompts and one 5–10 minute action with a grounding idea. This is built to be far from overwhelming, and sustainable for someone who can’t put their entire life on pause.

A crisis card for cravings

This is the most “clinical” exercise inside the entire workbook, but I promise it’s helpful. In this card, you’ll be prompted to think through certain scenarios and set up a game plan for yourself just in case you need it.

This might be my favorite part of the entire workbook. It’s created to help you start getting excited and prepared for life after you’re comfortable with sobriety. It includes career re-entry worksheets, a 90-day plan, social explanation scripts and some other tidbits.

Questions

What exactly do I get?

A 30-page PDF workbook, one page per day across four phases. Each page has one grounding idea, two reflection prompts, and one action that takes 5–10 minutes. Includes a crisis card and “Now What” worksheets for after day 30. Printable or fillable on-screen.

Do I need to be on day one?

No. The phases are built around common turning points in early sobriety, not a strict calendar. Jumping in on week two or three still works.

Is this a replacement for therapy or a treatment program?

No. It's a daily companion for the day-to-day moments that treatment doesn't always cover, built to sit alongside whatever support you already have. If you've been drinking heavily or daily, talk to a doctor before or as you stop.

Do I need to identify as an alcoholic to use this?

No labels required. The workbook is built for anyone with a question they're ready to take seriously, whatever form it's taken for them.
