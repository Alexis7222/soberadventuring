what’s inside

Four phases that move you!!!

Phase 01

Stabilize

Days 1–7

The first week is about getting your footing back: the prompts are to get the gears turning about the basics and refocusing the mind.

Phase 02

Rebuild Routine

Days 8–15

Once the worst of the physical stuff chills out, the second week rebuilds the scaffolding around your day so structure starts replacing what drinking used to fill.

Phase 03

Face the Hard Shit

Days 16–22

The third week stops avoiding what gets buried and you keep throwing sand over it. I’m not going to lie to you, this is the most challenging piece of the workbook.

Phase 04

Reconnect

Days 23–30

The last week brings it FULL circle by turning outward again by guiding you through prompts to rebuild the relationships and version of your life that substances took away.

how it works

One page a day, built to take about ten minutes, for thirty days.

  • 1

    A short idea and two reflection prompts

    Grounded in early recovery, specific enough to sit with for a second before you move on, without turning the page into a homework assignment.

  • 2

    One ten-minute action

    The page closes by asking you to do something specific, so thirty days adds up to more than thirty days of reading.

The structure repeats every day on purpose. By day five you’ll know exactly what to expect when you open the page, which matters most on the days you have the least energy to figure anything out.

questions

Before you check out.

Is this a replacement for therapy or a treatment program?

No. It’s a daily companion for the day-to-day moments treatment doesn’t always cover, built to sit alongside whatever support you already have.

What if I’m not on day one yet?

Start wherever you are. The phases are built around common turning points in early sobriety rather than a strict calendar, so jumping in on day eight or day twenty still works.
