Before you start

Is the challenge really free?

Yes, fully. The daily email challenge and community access are free. The PDF Toolkit is a separate paid product that goes deeper into the same material, with fillable pages built for working on paper.

What if I'm not on day one yet?

Start wherever you are. The phases are built around common turning points in early sobriety rather than a strict calendar, so jumping in on week two or three still works.

Is this a replacement for therapy or a treatment program?

No. It's a daily companion for the day-to-day moments that treatment doesn't always cover, and it's built to sit alongside whatever support you already have, not replace it.

Do I need to identify as an alcoholic to join?

No labels required, and that's on purpose. The challenge is built for women who have a question they're ready to take seriously, whatever form it's taken for them.
