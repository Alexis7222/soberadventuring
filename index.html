About Lexi

I built this because I needed both versions of that mirror.

I got sober at 25. I spent years in the version where something was nagging at me and I wasn't ready to name it, and then years in the version where I knew exactly what was happening and had very good explanations for why I had it handled. Neither version was the full truth, and nobody told me that both were allowed to be here at the same time. That's what The First 30 is.

Lexi Morgan · 2+ years sober · 4,000+ women through The First 30
