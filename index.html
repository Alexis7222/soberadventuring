let's get something straight

Everything you think
sobriety costs you is a lie.

I promise, it doesn't matter if you're sober, sober-curious, or just really done with the version of yourself that can't show up. You've been sold a story that isn't true, it may even be one of these:

the fun lie

"You'll never have fun at a party again."

You'll just stop pretending the party was fun to begin with.

the personality lie

"You'll lose your personality."

False. You'll finally meet her.

the networking lie

"Alcohol is required for networking, travel, and a real career."

I'm building a consulting business from Southeast Asia, sober, without a single networking happy hour. I've realized that the people worth knowing, will notice — and I'm willing to bet you'll realize that as well.

the employment lie

"No one will hire someone with your track record."

Stop trying to get hired and get clients. An employer may provide you benefits but your growth is capped and so is your pay. Freelancing cares about:

1. Do you have the confidence that you can deliver results?
2. Can you really deliver those results?

I'll show you exactly where to find the work and how to land it.

I am not a

the before

By 17, I'd already been arrested and wrecked over ten cars.
By 18, I hit a level of incomprehensible demoralization doing things for drugs that the sober version of me couldn't even imagine.
By 20, I'd burned through sixteen jobs and was deep in a benzo-opioid addiction with zero capacity to show up for anything.
By 22, dropped out of college for the third time with enough credits to practically have a master's degree.
By 23, hospitalized ten times. Survived my second suicide attempt.
By 24, totaled seven cars. (I don't drive anymore, as a public service.)

I've spiraled into 2 clinical, weed-induced psychoses lasting fourteen days with little to no sleep, convinced I was carrying baby Jesus while the government plotted to assassinate me. Also racked up $20k in debt. Paid it off in sobriety.

Two years later.

I'm a marketing and sales consultant, a mentor, and an artist. I'm two years sober, based in Chiang Mai, Thailand, with paying clients and a yummy delicious life that I designed on purpose, with initially, a ton of fear.

"Sobriety gave me back something I didn't know I'd lost, the ability to be awestruck and delighted by things. Nature. Playing board games with my family. A really good mocktail. A well made tiramisu."

My clients are enabled to step out of victimhood, whether that pertains to substance or career complacency, and find what they enjoy and are good at. I'm blessed to watch them lock in, apply themselves and make the money that allows them to travel, live abroad, or simply feel a deep sense of purpose in their work.
