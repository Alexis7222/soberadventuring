let's get something straight

Everything you think
sobriety costs you is a lie.

I promise, it doesn't matter if you're sober, sober-curious, or just really done with the version of yourself that can't show up. You've been sold a story that isn't true, it may even be one of these:

the fun lie

"You'll never have fun at a party again."

You'll just stop pretending the party was fun to begin with.

the personality lie

"You'll lose your personality."

So far from the truth! You're going to become the best version of you, promise.

the networking lie

"Alcohol is required for networking, travel, and a real career."

I'm building a consulting business from Southeast Asia, sober, without a single networking happy hour. I've realized that the people worth knowing, will notice, and I'm willing to bet you'll realize that as well.

the employment lie

"No one will hire someone with your track record."

Stop trying to get hired and get clients. An employer may provide you benefits but your growth is capped and so is your pay. Freelancing cares about:

1. Do you have the confidence that you can deliver results?
2. Can you really deliver those results?

I'll show you exactly where to find the work and how to land it.

Sobriety Coaching

The ALIVE Method.

The framework behind every sobriety coaching engagement. Practical, trauma-informed, and built for real life.

A

Assess Where are you actually stuck?

Internal (shame, identity, grief) or external (people, environment)? We figure this out first so nothing we do is wasted effort.

L

Locate Find your people

Isolation is the most common mistake in early sobriety. We identify who needs to go, who needs to come in, and where to find them.

I

Identity Not "I don't drink" but who you actually are

We develop this through narrative and belief work, motivational interviewing, and values exercises that build the person underneath the sobriety.

V

Village Structure the environment around the life you want

Routine and the physical and social spaces you move through.

E

Evolve Build a life you actually want to be present for

This is where it stops being about sobriety and starts being about your life.

Each phase has deliverables.
